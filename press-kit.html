Publishing Vision

About Sattvic Books

Wisdom Unfolding

Sattvic Books is a publishing and online bookstore initiative of The Sattvic Method Company. Inspired by India's rich literary, spiritual, cultural, and wellness traditions, Sattvic Books brings together publications that encourage conscious living, lifelong learning, cultural appreciation, and personal growth.

From Sanskrit literature and children's picture books to cookbooks, healing resources, spiritual guides, and journals, our collection aims to make meaningful knowledge accessible to readers of different ages and interests.

Our tagline: Wisdom Unfolding.

Author & Leadership

Founder & Author

Dr. Rani Iyer

Founder, Author & Creative Director

Dr. Rani Iyer is the founder and creative force behind The Sattvic Method Company and Sattvic Books. Her work explores the intersection of Indian knowledge traditions, spirituality, literature, conscious living, and education.

Through writing, publishing, educational initiatives, and community experiences, she seeks to make traditional knowledge approachable and meaningful for contemporary audiences.
