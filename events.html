Events at Sattvic Books

Discover Experiences Beyond Books

At Sattvic Books, we believe that learning doesn't end with reading a book. It continues through conversations, shared experiences, creative exploration, and meaningful connections.

Our events bring readers, authors, educators, and curious minds together to explore ideas, discover new perspectives, and experience the wisdom behind books in engaging ways.

From interactive workshops and poetry sessions to book discussions, storytelling, and spiritual learning experiences, every event offers an opportunity to learn something new and connect with a like-minded community.
