SUPPORT & POLICIES

Shipping & Returns

Information regarding digital fulfillment, electronic delivery, access support, and our digital book refund policy.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Published by Sattvic Books

Overview

Thank you for shopping with Sattvic Books. We are committed to providing a smooth and satisfying experience when purchasing our digital books.

As all products available on our website are digital books delivered electronically, there are no physical products to ship or return. Once a digital book has been purchased and delivered or made available for download, the order is generally considered final.

Please read our refund policy carefully before completing your purchase.

Digital Products – No Physical Shipping or Returns

All books purchased through Sattvic Books are digital products. They are delivered electronically through a download link or other digital access method provided after purchase.

  • No physical books or printed materials are shipped.
  • No shipping or delivery charges apply to digital book purchases unless otherwise specified.
  • Digital books cannot be physically returned.
  • Customers are responsible for providing a valid email address and ensuring they have the necessary device and software to access their purchased digital books.

Refund Eligibility

Due to the digital nature of our products, all sales are generally final once a digital book has been successfully delivered or made available for download.

However, we understand that certain situations may require special consideration. Refund requests may be reviewed under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate Purchase: You accidentally purchased the same digital book more than once.
  • Payment Error: You were charged multiple times for the same order.
  • Download or Access Issues: You are unable to access or download your purchased digital book due to a technical issue on our website.
  • Incorrect Product Delivery: You received a digital book different from the one you purchased.

Refund requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. We reserve the right to approve or decline a refund request depending on the circumstances.

Non-Refundable Purchases

Refunds will generally not be provided in the following situations:

  • You changed your mind after purchasing a digital book.
  • You purchased a digital book by mistake and the file has already been delivered or accessed.
  • You are dissatisfied with the content due to personal preferences or expectations that were not guaranteed in the product description.
  • You do not have the required device, software, or internet connection to access the digital book.
  • You have already downloaded or accessed the digital book and there is no verified technical or payment-related issue.

Refund Process

If you believe your purchase qualifies for a refund, please contact us at sattvicbooks@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Your full name.
  • Order number or purchase confirmation.
  • Email address used during checkout.
  • Name of the digital book purchased.
  • Reason for requesting a refund.
  • Relevant screenshots or other supporting information, if applicable.

Once we receive your request, our team will review the details and notify you by email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund.

If your refund is approved, it will be processed to your original payment method. The time required for the refund to appear in your account may vary depending on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider.

Late or Missing Refunds

If your refund has been approved but you have not received it yet, please follow these steps:

  1. Check your bank account or payment method again.
  2. Contact your credit card company or payment provider, as processing times may vary.
  3. Contact your bank to check whether the refund is still being processed.

If you have completed these steps and still have not received your refund, please contact us at sattvicbooks@gmail.com.

Digital Book Access & Technical Support

If you experience difficulties accessing, downloading, or opening a purchased digital book, please contact us at sattvicbooks@gmail.com.

Our team will review the issue and make reasonable efforts to help you access your purchase.

Please include your order number and a description of the issue so we can assist you more efficiently.

Gifts & Digital Book Purchases

If you purchase a digital book as a gift for someone else, please ensure that the recipient's email address is entered correctly wherever recipient details are requested during checkout.

Refund requests for gifted digital books will be reviewed according to the same refund eligibility conditions outlined in this policy.

Policy Updates

Sattvic Books reserves the right to update or modify this Digital Book Refund & Return Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page and will become effective upon posting.

We encourage customers to review this policy periodically.

Need Help?

If you have questions about digital book purchases, download access, payment issues, or refund requests, please contact us:

Email: sattvicbooks@gmail.com

We are happy to assist you and ensure you have a smooth experience with Sattvic Books.
