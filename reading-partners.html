Educational Collaboration

Learning Beyond the Pages

Reflect • Connect • Grow

Books become even more meaningful when readers have opportunities to reflect, ask questions, share ideas, and explore what they have learned.

Sattvic Books welcomes educational collaborations built around selected titles from our growing collection. We are happy to work with educators and community groups to develop resources that complement their reading programs and learning objectives.

Collaborative Paths

Our Reading Partnerships

For Teachers & Schools

Bring thoughtfully selected books into classroom learning through educational resources and classroom licensing arrangements.

For Libraries

Explore library purchases and opportunities to introduce readers to meaningful literature, children's books, and Indian cultural heritage.

For Reading Clubs

Create engaging group reading experiences with discussion prompts, guided exploration, and activities inspired by selected books.

For Balavihar Groups

Explore stories, literature, Sanskrit, Indian traditions, and timeless values through age-appropriate reading and learning activities.

For Cooking Clubs & Food Communities

Welcome cooking clubs, culinary educators, and food communities to explore conscious cooking and Sattvic food traditions through guided reading and recipe activities.

Custom Resources

Customized Reading Guides & Lesson Plans

Depending on the book and the group's needs, custom educational resources may include:

Reading guides to help readers engage with stories and ideas.

Discussion questions that encourage reflection, curiosity, and meaningful conversations.

Sanskrit vocabulary and pronunciation activities to introduce readers to the beauty of the language.

Storytelling activities that bring characters, settings, and ideas to life.

Creative writing exercises to encourage imagination and self-expression.

Cultural exploration activities that introduce readers to India's literary and cultural heritage.

Lesson plans to help educators incorporate selected books into their learning programs.

Our intention is to support educators and community leaders with resources that make reading engaging, accessible, and rewarding.
