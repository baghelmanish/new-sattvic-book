Gita Weekend Circle

Thirty quiet minutes
with the Gita.

Read in silence. Write from what stirs. Once a week, together with readers everywhere.

No fee No pressure No experience needed
Register free How it works

Each session needs a quick sign-in so we can send your link and your free Gita PDF.

Free & open to all  ·  Begins on Gita Jayanti, Sunday, December 20, 2026

Two sessions. One Sunday.

Begin the day with your pen, close it with the page. Join one, or keep both as your weekend ritual.

Morning · 30 min

Gita Inspired Writing

Sunday, 7:30 AM CT

Take one verse or idea from your reading and write whatever it opens: a poem, a letter, a paragraph, a question. Share a line if you wish, or keep it private.

Evening · 30 min

Silent Gita Reading

Sunday, 5:30 PM CT

Cameras optional, mics off. Read any chapter you choose, at your own pace. Tell us which chapter on the sign-in form. That is all.

Come to one or both. You register each time, which keeps the circle warm and personal.

What you receive

A free Gita PDF

Every participant gets a free Gita book PDF from Sattvic Books.

A steady rhythm

Thirty minutes of calm that fits a weekend, with company.

A place to be heard

Your writing may be featured in the Sattvic Books community, with your permission.
