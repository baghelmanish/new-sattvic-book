Thank you for choosing Sattvic Books for your digital workshop and book needs. Please review our Return and Shipping Policy below:

Returns

As a digital workshop and book-selling company, we do not accept returns for our digital products including workshops and e-books. Once purchased, these items are non-refundable. We encourage customers to review product details and descriptions carefully before making a purchase decision.

In the event of a technical issue or if you encounter any problems accessing your purchased digital workshop or e-book, please contact our customer support team at Sattvic Books for assistance. We are committed to resolving any issues promptly and ensuring your satisfaction with our products.

Shipping

As all our products are digital, there is no physical shipping involved. Upon successful completion of your purchase, you will receive an email confirmation containing instructions on how to access and download your digital workshop or e-book. Please ensure to check your email inbox as well as your spam or junk folder to locate the email.

Cancellation

We understand that circumstances may change, and you may need to cancel your order. As our products are digital and accessible immediately upon purchase, cancellations are not typically possible. However, if you encounter any issues or have specific concerns regarding your order, please reach out to our customer support team, and we will do our best to assist you.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or require further clarification regarding our Return and Shipping Policy, please don’t hesitate to contact us at sattvicbooks@gmail.com. Our customer support team is available to assist you and ensure your experience with Sattvic Books is a positive one.

Thank you for choosing Sattvic Books for your digital workshop and book needs. We appreciate your business and look forward to serving you.

Sincerely,

Sattvic Books
