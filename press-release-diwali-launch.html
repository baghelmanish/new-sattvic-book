Sattvic Books Launches This Diwali: A Digital Home for Indian Wisdom, Literature, and Meaningful Reading

A new publishing and educational initiative from The Sattvic Method Company brings together Indian literature, children's books, spirituality, conscious living, and learning resources for every generation.

THIS DIWALI, LIGHT AN INNER LIBRARY.

A world of wisdom. A lifetime of stories. Something meaningful for every generation.

INTRODUCING SATTVIC BOOKS • Read. Reflect. Grow.
Official Announcement November 9, 2026 SAN ANTONIO, Texas
Media & Press Press Kit

SAN ANTONIO, Texas — This Diwali, as homes and communities celebrate the triumph of light over darkness, The Sattvic Method Company announces the inauguration and official website launch of Sattvic Books, a digital-first publishing and learning destination dedicated to books that illuminate the mind, nurture the heart, and inspire conscious living.

Established as a subsidiary of The Sattvic Method Company, Sattvic Books represents a new chapter in the company's commitment to making meaningful knowledge, timeless wisdom, and transformative reading accessible to readers of all ages.

Its thoughtfully curated collection spans Indian philosophy and spirituality, Bhagavad Gita wisdom, healing and wellness, conscious cooking, pooja and ritual guides, journals, reflective reading, children's literature, poetry, fiction, and practical resources for everyday life.

A Diwali Inauguration

Sattvic Books will be officially inaugurated and its website launched on Monday, November 9, 2026, at 9:00 AM Indian Standard Time (IST), corresponding to Sunday, November 8, 2026, at 10:30 PM Central Time (USA).

The launch is timed to coincide with Diwali Amavasya, the sacred new-moon night celebrated as the Festival of Lights. On this auspicious occasion, Sattvic Books opens its doors with a vision of bringing the light of knowledge, literature, and timeless wisdom into homes across generations.

Just as a lamp dispels darkness, a meaningful book can illuminate the mind and transform the way we see the world.

A Digital Library, Accessible Across Generations

At launch, Sattvic Books will offer digital books and learning resources, making its publications accessible to readers across geographical boundaries. New titles will initially be available exclusively through the Sattvic Books website.

The collection is being developed for readers of different ages and interests, with books exploring Indian literature, spirituality, philosophy, children's stories, conscious cooking, healing, and personal growth. Selected free books and learning resources will also be available.

By making its publications available digitally, Sattvic Books seeks to make meaningful reading and learning resources accessible to families, educators, and communities around the world.

More Than a Bookstore: A Place for Every Generation

Sattvic Books believes that a good book can do much more than entertain. It can introduce a child to the richness of a story, help a family preserve its cultural heritage, offer practical knowledge for everyday living, or open a doorway to deeper self-understanding.

For children: Beautifully conceived stories and illustrated books that introduce India's literary and cultural heritage, imagination, values, and timeless wisdom.

For families: Cookbooks, practical guides, and resources that encourage mindful food choices, healthier routines, and conscious living.

For seekers of wisdom: Books exploring Hindu philosophy, Sanskrit literature, sacred traditions, spiritual practices, and the Bhagavad Gita.

For personal growth: Healing books, reflective journals, and resources that encourage self-discovery, inner clarity, and well-being.

Featured Publications

Among the distinctive titles in the Sattvic Books collection are publications that bring India's literary and cultural heritage to contemporary readers.

Meghadootam: The Cloud Messenger
The Rice That Grew
Voices in Verse
Gauri Ganesh

Meghadootam: The Cloud Messenger (ISBN: 978-1-967672-23-3) is a bilingual picture book in Sanskrit and English, introducing readers to the celebrated Sanskrit classic Meghadutam by the great poet Kalidasa. Through an accessible bilingual presentation and visual storytelling, this timeless work invites children and families to discover the beauty, imagery, and lyrical richness of classical Sanskrit poetry.

The Rice That Grew: A Story from Ancient India, retold by Dr. Rani Iyer from a story in Dandin's Dashakumaracharita, brings an engaging classical Indian tale to younger readers.

Voices in Verse: 21 Poets Who Built a Language of Beauty celebrates the literary contributions of poets who shaped India's rich traditions of language and poetry.

Gauri Ganesh: A Shining Song for Little Hearts introduces young readers to devotional music and the joy of traditional lyrics.

The collection also includes books on spirituality, philosophy, conscious cooking, healing, and personal growth, reflecting Sattvic Books' commitment to making meaningful reading accessible to every generation.

Inauguration Offer

A Diwali Gift to Readers: 10% Off All Books

To celebrate the inauguration, Sattvic Books is offering readers a special 10% discount on all books from November 9, 2026, through January 14, 2027, concluding on the occasion of Makar Sankranti.

Whether readers are looking for a thoughtful holiday gift, an engaging book for a child, a practical cookbook, or a meaningful addition to their personal library, this is an opportunity to discover something that can continue giving long after the festive season.

A Growing Community Beyond Books

The inauguration marks only the beginning. Sattvic Books plans to expand its offerings with an online gift shop, a book club, reading initiatives, literary activities, and other opportunities that bring readers together.

The vision is to create a vibrant community where books become the starting point for conversations, learning, creativity, and shared experiences across generations.

The new venture will also complement the wider educational and community initiatives of The Sattvic Method Company, which offers free activities, learning resources, online sessions, and programs designed to encourage conscious living and personal growth. Through this relationship, readers will have opportunities to go beyond reading and put knowledge into practice through accessible learning experiences and community activities.

Sattvic Books welcomes partnerships with schools, libraries, teachers, reading clubs, cooking clubs, and Balavihar groups around the world.

Educators and institutions may contact Sattvic Books for classroom licensing and library purchases. The initiative also welcomes collaborations with reading groups and educational communities interested in exploring its publications through structured learning experiences.

For a nominal fee, Sattvic Books is happy to work with teachers, reading clubs, and Balavihar groups to develop customized reading guides, discussion resources, and lesson plans around selected books.

These resources may include age-appropriate discussion questions, Sanskrit vocabulary and pronunciation activities, storytelling exercises, creative writing, and opportunities to explore India's literary and cultural heritage.

The aim is to make reading a shared journey—encouraging children and adults to engage with books, exchange ideas, discover cultural traditions, and develop a lasting love of learning.

The Vision Behind Sattvic Books

Dr. Rani Iyer Founder & Author

Founded by Dr. Rani Iyer, Sattvic Books grew out of a vision to make timeless Indian wisdom, meaningful literature, and practical knowledge accessible to contemporary families.

Bringing together a love of books, cultural preservation, education, and conscious living, Dr. Iyer envisions Sattvic Books as a growing collection of resources that can inspire readers across generations.

Through Sattvic Books and The Sattvic Method Company, this vision extends beyond publications to include learning experiences, educational collaborations, and opportunities for families and communities to explore knowledge together.
