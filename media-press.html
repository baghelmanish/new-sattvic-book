Media & Press

Sattvic Books: Bringing the Light of Wisdom to Every Home

A new publishing destination for timeless Indian wisdom, meaningful stories, conscious living, and lifelong learning.

This Diwali, Sattvic Books celebrates the launch of its online bookstore, bringing together a thoughtfully curated collection of books inspired by India's rich literary, cultural, spiritual, and wellness traditions.

Rooted in the belief that books can illuminate, inspire, and transform lives, Sattvic Books seeks to make timeless knowledge accessible to contemporary readers, families, educators, and seekers of wisdom.

Our collection spans Sanskrit literature, Hindu philosophy, children's stories, cookbooks, healing and wellness, spiritual practices, journals, and meaningful resources for everyday living.

Our Vision & Heritage

Our Story

Ancient Wisdom • Modern Life

Sattvic Books is an extension of The Sattvic Method Company's vision of making traditional Indian knowledge relevant and accessible in modern life.

We believe that reading is more than acquiring information. It is an opportunity to cultivate awareness, preserve cultural heritage, nurture imagination, and pass meaningful knowledge from one generation to the next.

Through original publications, thoughtfully developed adaptations, practical guides, and curated reading resources, we aim to create a publishing space where ancient wisdom meets contemporary curiosity.

Distinctive Values

What Makes Sattvic Books Distinctive?

Indian Knowledge Traditions

Books inspired by Sanskrit literature, Indian philosophy, spiritual traditions, and cultural heritage.

Stories for Young Readers

Engaging, illustrated stories that introduce children to India's literary treasures, values, traditions, and imagination.

Conscious Living & Wellness

Resources exploring mindful living, healing, self-development, and holistic well-being.

Food, Culture & Cooking

Cookbooks, sattvic food traditions, recipes, and cooking clubs that bring people together through mindful food and shared learning.

Practical Spiritual Resources

Guides, journals, ritual resources, and publications that help readers bring meaningful practices into everyday life.

Festival of Lights

Our Diwali Launch

Diwali celebrates the triumph of light over darkness and knowledge over ignorance. The launch of Sattvic Books during this auspicious season reflects our aspiration to make the light of knowledge available to more readers.

Our invitation is simple: discover a book, explore an idea, share a story, and welcome more wisdom into your life.
